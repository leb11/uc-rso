About UC-IRPA

Institutional Research, Planning and Analytics

UC-IRPA is formerly known as UC – Research Services Office (RSO).

We support evidence-based planning and impactful research that protects lives, water, health, and livelihoods across the Cordillera. Our office guides researchers, delivers GIS and statistical analytics, and connects initiatives with funding and institutional partners.

What guides our work

  • Useful Research

    Practical studies aligned with institutional planning and regional needs.

  • Community Safety

    Data-driven solutions for disaster resilience, health, and water security.

  • Open Collaboration

    Strong partnerships across universities, LGUs, and national agencies.

  • Capacity Building

    Mentoring, tools, and analytics support for every UCian researcher.
