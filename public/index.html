About us

Research with a purpose

UC-RSO supports research that protects lives, water, health, and livelihoods in the Cordillera. We guide researchers, run GIS and statistical work, and connect projects with funding and partners.

What guides us

Useful research. Community safety. Open collaboration. Capacity building for every UCian researcher.
